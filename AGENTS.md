# Prototype Instructions

Run the local server yourself and open the preview in the browser available to this environment. Do not give the user server-start instructions when you can run it.

Every completed application change must be committed, pushed to the connected GitHub repository, and deployed to the linked Vercel production project after validation; do not leave completed app changes only in the local worktree.

Before making substantial visual changes, use the Product Design plugin's `get-context` skill when the visual source is unclear or no longer matches the current goal. When the user gives durable prototype-specific design feedback, preferences, or decisions, record them in `AGENTS.md`.

When implementing from a selected generated mock, treat that image as the source of truth for layout, component anatomy, density, spacing, color, typography, visible content, and hierarchy.

## Product decisions

- The selected visual target is option 2 from the 27 July 2026 ideation set: a dense fleet ledger with a right-side vehicle inspector.
- The operating fleet has exactly five vehicles: three professional and two particular.
- Every vehicle has two assigned drivers; the fleet has ten unique drivers.
- Professional vehicles receive two WhatsApp shift reports per day. Each report records driver, start and accumulated odometer, kilometres driven, fuel litres, cost, timestamp, extraction confidence, and any consumption anomaly.
- Particular vehicles do not require per-shift reporting; either assigned driver can submit a shared odometer reading.
- Vehicle detail defaults to a collapsible shift ledger so the manager can audit each daily turn without leaving the fleet screen.
- The main ledger omits separate Vehicle and Status columns. The model remains visible below the plate, while Status is replaced by a numeric remaining-kilometres countdown to the next service.
- The Vehículos ledger labels the accumulated vehicle odometer column “Km totales”, never “Km acumulados”.
- Driver names are interactive selectors. Selecting either driver must update that row and the inspector with the driver's kilometres and fuel spend in euros for the day.
- The main ledger has no Uso column. Facturación appears immediately after Conductores.
- Driver selection must update daily billing, monthly accumulated billing, monthly accumulated trips, and the portion collected in cash today. Particular drivers display zero billing and zero trips unless they are later assigned commercial activity.
- The ledger includes a Taller column with the latest maintenance amount and concept. Selecting it navigates to the selected vehicle's dated workshop history inside Mantenimiento.
- The first release is a functional front-end prototype with realistic local data; WhatsApp, email, OpenAI extraction, authentication, and persistence remain simulated.
- The web client is an installable PWA. Production must ship a linked manifest, 192px and 512px install icons, maskable icons, `display: standalone`, a root-scoped service worker, and an in-app install action when the browser exposes the install prompt.
- The product name is SOBRE RUEDAS and the primary interface language is Spanish. Its company/project logo is the exact user-supplied red-and-white wheel artwork stored at `public/brand/sobre-ruedas-logo.png`; the desktop home control uses that image without reinterpretation, while the phone home control and installable PWA icon use a centered square, safe-area adaptation stored at `public/brand/sobre-ruedas-app-icon.png`.
- The phone launcher, favicon, Apple touch icon, manifest icons, maskable icons, and manifest shortcuts must always use the red SOBRE RUEDAS wheel artwork, never the legacy T icon. Icon URLs are versioned whenever the artwork changes so installed mobile clients fetch the replacement.
- Every destination is a real application window. Flota, Lecturas, Facturas, Mantenimiento, Automatizaciones, Ajustes, and Ayuda must change the route hash, title, content, and active navigation state, but they are entered only from SOBRE RUEDAS controls.
- There is no permanent sidebar. The unnamed SOBRE RUEDAS summary is the sole navigation hub. Its first quick-action row shows only Vehículos and Facturas; Lecturas must not appear in that row. The persistent tyre-logo button always returns to this summary, including from an open KPI detail, and restores the combined four-colour chart with one bar per vehicle.
- Vehículos is the single consolidated operational workspace. The former Vehículos, Facturación, Combustible/Repostaje, and Consumo mensual por vehículo entry points route to one `#/flota` screen using the Combustible banner-and-ledger pattern, with vehicle operations, monthly billing, refuelling history, and monthly consumption shown together. A legacy `#/gasolina` link resolves to Vehículos.
- Product hierarchy follows the selected dense-ledger direction with stronger readable type, elevated KPI cards, a persistent operational table, and an optional right-side inspector. On mobile, the inspector opens only after a vehicle is selected.
- Core review actions use accessible dialogs, visible focus states, semantic tables, named controls, and Escape-to-close behavior.
- Taller is integrated into Mantenimiento and is reached from the General quick actions. It offers a vehicle selector, full history, repeated concepts, and a photo-to-invoice action that preselects the current vehicle.
- Facturas created from a workshop photo are kept locally between reloads and immediately update Facturas, the selected vehicle's maintenance history, its July workshop expense, and its calculated profit margin.
- The vehicle inspector contains Actividad, Mantenimiento, Gasolina, and Gastos. Mantenimiento uses a compact Excel-like grid with one row for each agreed maintenance concept, the latest date, and a control to view the associated invoice document. Gasolina lists each driver's daily litres and fuel spend for the selected vehicle.
- Vehículos is a full fleet page, not only an inspector tab. It shows the current month's vehicle totals, monthly billing, accumulated litres and spend for all five vehicles, followed by the operational ledger, the selected vehicle's dated refuelling ledger, and the integrated billing workspace. Professional refuellings are assigned automatically to a driver from the recorded time and the vehicle's configured shift schedule.
- The Vehículos workspace uses the selected mobile reports visual language: a black global top bar over the teal report canvas, no visible report-tab row or standalone operational-entry/date row, stacked horizontal vehicle banners, anchored month selector, and a combined vehicle/facturación/consumo ledger. The tyre-logo button returns to General. Repostaje and Facturación are sections of Vehículos rather than separate entry points.
- The dashboard formerly called Informes is the default "SOBRE RUEDAS" home view for the root URL and installed PWA. It opens on General and shows SOBRE RUEDAS in the top bar. The Vehículos quick action is the single entry point for the combined vehicle workspace.
- Every root launch is normalized to `#/informes`. A standalone PWA launch always resets to SOBRE RUEDAS > General even when the previous session closed on another route; direct non-standalone deep links remain available.
- The global top bar is black with the red SOBRE RUEDAS wheel and white title. The notification bell remains visible, while a three-line menu reveals the Automatizaciones, Ajustes, and Ayuda icons.
- The SOBRE RUEDAS home dashboard has four financial metrics: Facturación in light green, Mantenimiento in orange, Combustible in red, and Neto in green. Their totals must represent monthly driver billing, workshop spend, recorded fuel spend, and billing minus all vehicle expenses respectively.
- General uses one elongated blue Conductores button that opens the driver analytics workspace; Mantenimiento is the second elongated button and Neto appears below it. Facturas is available from the black top bar immediately to the left of the notification bell.
- The grouped Vehículos button is the only dashboard entry point for the combined vehicle, billing, and refuelling workspace. The duplicate Repostaje, Ingreso, Conductores, and Combustible quick actions remain removed, and the General dashboard fits the viewport without vertical page scrolling.
- The orange Mantenimiento button is the only visible entry point for the complete maintenance workspace from General. The duplicate Mantenimiento quick action is removed.
- The Neto button retains its dedicated chart interaction and opens the Gasto detail when selected through its chart flow.
- The phrase "Control de flota" is not used in the interface. The Flota workspace is headed simply "Vehículos".
- Vehicle order is fixed everywhere: 1) 5043 MLC, professional, Toyota Corolla; 2) 5750 MJV, professional, Toyota Corolla; 3) 5754 MJV, professional, Toyota Corolla; 4) 0344 LCP, particular, Lexus IS 300h; 5) 9401 LTG, particular, Peugeot 2008.
- Professional schedules are 5043 MLC (Mauricio 07:00–19:00, Amin 19:00–07:00), 5750 MJV (Tirso 06:00–18:00, Alex 18:00–06:00), and 5754 MJV (Carlos 04:00–16:00, Fernando 16:00–04:00).
- The maintenance concept grid contains Aceite y filtro, Filtro habitáculo, Filtro de aire, Neumáticos, Pastillas de freno, Discos de freno, Transmisión, Bomba de agua, Bujías, Aceite de caja de cambios, Limpiaparabrisas, Fundas de asientos, and Varios.
- Every concept listed under a dated maintenance event's "Trabajos realizados" is interactive. Selecting it expands an inline table containing every exact normalized match for that concept across all dated work records for the currently selected vehicle, including date, odometer, concept, and amount.
- Mantenimiento opens with five stacked horizontal vehicle banners in fixed fleet order. Each banner shows the real Toyota, Lexus, or Peugeot brand mark before the plate and model. Selecting a banner reveals that vehicle's interventions ordered newest-first with dates formatted YYYY/MM/DD. The date control independently expands the work performed, while a separate invoice control opens the workshop invoice when one is available.
- Gastos shows vehicle-specific amounts for leasing coche, préstamo licencia, gasolina, taller, seguridad social, nómina, comisiones conductor, impuestos trimestrales, IVA intracomunitario, seguro, limpieza coche, and varios.
- Gastos calculates each vehicle's monthly profit margin as the combined monthly billing of its two drivers minus every expense assigned to that vehicle. The UI shows both driver subtotals, total billing, total expenses, absolute margin, and margin percentage.
- The General report chart uses one stacked bar per vehicle. Each bar exposes the four established color-coded metrics: Facturación (light green), Mantenimiento (orange), Combustible (red), and Neto (green), while the grouped dashboard buttons preserve the operational routes and the Neto detail interaction.
- Uber Developer integration is server-only: UBER_CLIENT_ID, UBER_CLIENT_SECRET, UBER_REDIRECT_URI, and UBER_SCOPES are environment variables; secrets are never rendered in the browser, stored in localStorage, or committed. The connection uses Uber Driver API OAuth and only requests partner.accounts, partner.payments, and partner.trips. A diagnostics action reports endpoint availability and counts without returning bearer tokens or personal payloads.
- The public Uber privacy-policy route is /privacidad. It documents the requested Uber data, purposes, server-side retention, sharing, deletion rights, contact email, and OAuth disconnection. Settings exposes the same policy link and a server-side disconnect action clears the encrypted OAuth session cookie.
- The General chart header shows only “Resumen general por coche”; it does not show an explanatory subtitle below the title.
- Vehicle registration plates shown along the bottom axis of the General, Mantenimiento, Combustible, and Neto charts use bold type; driver names in the Facturación chart retain regular weight.
- The Ingreso report keeps exactly four concepts visually grouped in a centered compact table: Conductor, Vehículo, Viajes, and Ingreso mensual. Turno and its schedule must not appear in this view.
- Driver names throughout the Facturación workspace use uppercase text and a larger type size than the surrounding vehicle, trip, schedule, and revenue details.
- Every professional driver name in Facturación is an interactive selector. Selecting one opens an inline Monday-first calendar synchronized with the Facturación month and year controls; every billed day shows its daily revenue directly below the date, and the daily amounts add up exactly to that driver's monthly billing.
- Each professional vehicle plate in Facturación is also an interactive selector. Selecting it reveals the combined monthly billing of its two associated drivers, with each driver's subtotal and the vehicle total.
- The Facturación workspace header shows only the centered light-green title “Facturación”; the previous two-line income heading and explanatory subtitle are removed.
- Mantenimiento separates its five vehicle banners from the selected vehicle history with a black horizontal divider.
- When a KPI detail is open, the top bar becomes a compact title bar containing only the SOBRE RUEDAS wheel control and the centered name of the active detail: Mantenimiento, Facturación, Combustible, or Neto.
- The Ajustes action bar containing Descartar and Guardar ajustes remains fixed to the bottom of the viewport while the settings content scrolls, with reserved page space so it never covers the last controls.
- The Repostaje detail ledger uses this exact column order: Fecha, Hora, Conductor, Importe, Precio/Litro, and Factura. Turno and Litros do not occupy separate columns. Every entry includes a button that opens its locally archived Plenergy invoice, representing a document downloaded from the fleet's Plenergy application account.
- Combustible uses the same compact anchored month selector pattern as Mantenimiento: all twelve months appear in one vertical menu, the menu closes after selection, on focus leaving, or with Escape, and the selected period updates vehicle totals and the daily refuelling ledger.
- The Combustible vehicle selector mirrors Mantenimiento with five stacked horizontal banners in fixed fleet order. Each banner shows the vehicle number, real brand mark, brand, plate, model, and current-period fuel consumption on the right. Selecting a banner reveals that vehicle's refuellings sorted newest-first, with a receipt/invoice action at the far right of every row.
- When the Conductores button opens the driver analytics workspace, it shows compact full-width Facturación and Consumo summaries, followed by six borderless full-width driver rows without avatars or arrows; billing and consumption amounts stay prominent, and selecting a driver scrolls to that driver's calendar.
- Conductores keeps only euro amounts in the Consumo summary (no per-vehicle litres), uses bold enlarged vehicle plates and metrics, shows complete driver labels without clipping, and presents the selected calendar with only the driver name and month. The daily detail heading is omitted; Facturación/Repostaje panel headings and calendar-day amounts use enlarged type.
- Selecting a Toyota vehicle in the unified Vehículos workspace reveals both assigned drivers directly beneath its banner; particular vehicles remain without driver names.
- The particular Lexus 0344 LCP and Peugeot 9401 LTG do not display driver names or driver selectors in Flota or in the vehicle inspector; both are presented as vehicles without an associated driver.
- The SOBRE RUEDAS General screen opens with a combined four-colour summary chart for Facturación, Mantenimiento, Combustible, and Neto. Its grouped Vehículos button routes to the combined operational workspace; Mantenimiento opens its workspace and Neto retains its dedicated chart interaction. A Resumen control restores the combined chart, and a shared month and year selector updates the active chart and dashboard totals. The month menu keeps all twelve months in one vertical column, is right-aligned to its trigger, and uses only the width required by the month labels.
- The General chart metric control is an anchored dropdown with Resumen, Facturación, Mantenimiento, Combustible, and Neto options; Resumen is selected by default and combines all four series. Mantenimiento opens directly on its vehicle banners without the preventive-plan intro copy.
- The three-line management dropdown closes when the user clicks outside it, and its Facturas route button remains in the black global top bar next to the bell.
- The General home screen is a fixed no-scroll, no-zoom single-screen dashboard: the Conductores card has no arrow, Mantenimiento and Neto use compact uppercase title bands with a continuous Total/Por día/Por km metric row, and Mantenimiento is orange in the summary chart.
- On the General home screen, the Conductores card matches the compact height of Mantenimiento and Neto. The Resumen general por coche heading is uppercase and bold, and its Resumen, Mes, and Año controls use enlarged uppercase text across their available boxes without clipping.
- The three top-bar controls are compact, the three General dashboard buttons are 50% narrower on desktop and full-width on mobile, Mantenimiento uses orange, period menus present months and years as a vertical wheel with scroll snapping, and the Vehículos workspace omits the three bottom vehicle-driver summary cards.
- Month and year are selected through compact anchored in-page menus, never full-screen or browser-native pickers. The month menu shows all 12 months in one vertical column, the year menu uses a short list, and both close after selection, on focus leaving the control, or with Escape.

Build app UI in `src/`. Keep `.openai/hosting.json`, `worker/index.js`, `scripts/prepare-sites-build.mjs`, and `tests/sites-worker.test.mjs` intact so the same local prototype can be handed to Sites. Before a Sites handoff, run `npm run build` and `npm run test:sites`; the build must leave `dist/client/index.html`, `dist/server/index.js`, and `dist/.openai/hosting.json`.
